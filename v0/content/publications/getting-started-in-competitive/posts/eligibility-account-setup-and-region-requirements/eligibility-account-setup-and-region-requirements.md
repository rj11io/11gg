# Eligibility, account setup and region requirements

Open the event you want to play before assuming your account is eligible. A locked poster can mean either missing requirements or a session that has not started. Epic directs players to the lock icon and **DETAILS** for the specific conditions and their progress. [Epic tournament entry help](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-34254770/c-45529661/a11282128).

**Rules checked October 4, 2026.** This guide uses the master rules dated June 4 and named 2026 event examples. Those examples explain how requirements differ; check your event's current rules and in-game entry panel before joining. Continue the curriculum through [Getting started in competitive: start here]({{READING_GUIDE}}).

## Age, consent and account ownership

Epic's minimum competitive age is 13, or a higher minimum required where you live. Minors need parent or guardian permission and acceptance of the rules; the master rules use 18 or the local age of majority. Keep your account's age information accurate. [Epic age requirements](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-34254770/a13632224), [master rules, sections 1.3 and 3.1](https://www.fortnite.com/competitive/rules-guidelines/rules-library/fortnite-competitive-master-rules?region=NAC).

Cabined Accounts cannot enter. Use your own correctly registered account in good standing; transferred accounts and applicable sanctions can prevent participation. Check the full eligibility section, including affiliation restrictions. [Master rules, sections 3.1–3.6](https://www.fortnite.com/competitive/rules-guidelines/rules-library/fortnite-competitive-master-rules?region=NAC).

## Enable 2FA on the account you play

Two-factor authentication is required for competitive events. In your Epic account's **Password and security** page, choose **Set up** under 2FA, follow the selected method's instructions and confirm that it is active. Epic supports codes through email, SMS or an authenticator app. [Epic 2FA setup help](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-45487929/c-38978022/2fa-a10075388).

If Fortnite still reports 2FA as inactive, check that you enabled it on the Epic account used by the game. Epic also identifies unfinished console-linked accounts without an assigned email as a possible cause. Follow its account setup instructions for that case. [Epic 2FA recognition help](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-45487929/c-38978022/a25139225).

## Account level, rank and qualification

The June 4 master rules require **Career account level 350** for FNCS, Cash Cups and Performance Evaluations, and **50** for other competitions. Check the account level in Career, then check additional event gates. [Master rules, section 3.6.3](https://www.fortnite.com/competitive/rules-guidelines/rules-library/fortnite-competitive-master-rules?region=NAC).

There is no single Ranked threshold to copy across every tournament. Read the named event's requirements. Two examples from the checked rules:

| Named event | Additional entry condition in its rules |
| --- | --- |
| FNCS Solos 2026 | Qualifier Round 1 requires top 200,000 in the overall official Power Rankings leaderboard. Top 2,000 may start in Round 2; entering Round 1 forfeits that automatic Round 2 place. Later rounds require advancement. |
| Arenas Performance Evaluation, Chapter 7 Season 4 | Solo Arenas in EU only, requiring top 1,000 in the overall official Power Rankings leaderboard. |

These conditions come from [FNCS Solos, sections 2.2.2–2.2.3](https://www.fortnite.com/competitive/rules-guidelines/rules-library/fortnite-championship-series-fncs-solos-2026-official-rules?region=NAC) and [Arenas Performance Evaluation, section 2.2.1](https://www.fortnite.com/competitive/rules-guidelines/rules-library/arenas-performance-evaluation-chapter-7-season-4-official-rules?region=NAC). Official Epic Power Rankings and your Ranked tier are separate measures. Use the official measure named by the event. [Epic Power Rankings explanation](https://www.fortnite.com/competitive/power-rankings-information?region=EU).

### The 14-tournament condition needs an event check

Master section 3.6.5 expressly requires FNCS players to have entered 14 distinct Epic tournaments in the preceding 180 days. Epic's help pages describe that condition more broadly for cash or 350-level events. Their wording differs, so do not infer that another event waives it. Check that event's rules and entry panel. [Master rules](https://www.fortnite.com/competitive/rules-guidelines/rules-library/fortnite-competitive-master-rules?region=NAC), [entry help](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-34254770/c-45529661/a11282128), [tournament counting help](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-34254770/a19930759).

Repeated rounds or regions of the same tournament do not count as separate tournaments. Epic's counting help lists Ranked Cups, OG Cups and Mobile Series as examples that contribute; check their own eligibility and availability before entering. [Tournament counting help](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-34254770/a19930759).

## Platform and team checks

Events are generally cross-platform, with designated platform or console exceptions. Australia residents cannot compete on iOS under the checked master rules. One player per device during an event and one account entry per session apply. [Master rules, sections 2.3 and 3.7](https://www.fortnite.com/competitive/rules-guidelines/rules-library/fortnite-competitive-master-rules?region=NAC).

Check the format and permitted hardware for the named event. For example, the PlayStation NickEh30 Icon Cup has separate Solo Battle Royale and Zero Build qualifiers, then requires top-100 regional placement in the applicable mode for its final. Confirm the designated console in the entry panel; the rules body does not list console models. [NickEh30 Cup, section 2.2.1](https://www.fortnite.com/competitive/rules-guidelines/rules-library/playstation-nick-eh-30-cup?region=NAC).

For team events, check every member's eligibility and the roster rules. Master section 3.7.6 sets competition-specific team locks and an FNCS LCQ teammate-change exception. Read the stages applicable to your event; FNCS Solos uses a solo format. [Master rules](https://www.fortnite.com/competitive/rules-guidelines/rules-library/fortnite-competitive-master-rules?region=NAC), [FNCS Solos](https://www.fortnite.com/competitive/rules-guidelines/rules-library/fortnite-championship-series-fncs-solos-2026-official-rules?region=NAC).

## Choose a region before the first session

Epic's settings instructions are **player icon → Settings → Game → Language and Region → Matchmaking Region**, then **Apply**. Auto chooses the best ping and fixes Subregion Preference to Auto; selecting a specific region allows a subregion preference. Check the event's competitive region separately. [Epic region settings help](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-37330187/c-35433838/a10221394?lang=en-US).

FNCS Solos 2026 locks your account to one region for the entire event once you participate in a Qualifier Round 1 or Round 2 session. Changing the server setting does not remove that event lock. [FNCS Solos, section 4.1](https://www.fortnite.com/competitive/rules-guidelines/rules-library/fortnite-championship-series-fncs-solos-2026-official-rules?region=NAC).

Residence-based participation restrictions and prize restrictions are separate from server selection. Master section 3.7.1 lists prohibited locations; section 2.5.1 restricts cash prizes for Turkey and Russia residents. Read the named event's prize wording too. [Master rules](https://www.fortnite.com/competitive/rules-guidelines/rules-library/fortnite-competitive-master-rules?region=NAC).

## When the event is still locked

Use the missing condition to choose the next check:

| What you see | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| A lock before the session | The start time and listed requirements in Compete; a lock alone does not identify the cause. |
| 2FA is missing | The Epic account used by Fortnite, its active 2FA method and any unfinished linked-account setup. |
| Qualification is missing | The selected round, prior advancement and any tournament-history condition. Repeating one event's rounds does not multiply its history count. |
| Your PR seems eligible but the poster disagrees | Epic says PR eligibility updates around one week before the tournament; an earlier display can be inaccurate. Inspect DETAILS near the event. |
| Entry remains blocked after a sanction | Check the applicable competitive sanction rules and Epic's entry guidance. |

These checks follow [Epic entry help](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-34254770/c-45529661/a11282128), [2FA recognition help](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-45487929/c-38978022/a25139225) and [tournament counting help](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-34254770/a19930759). If a listed condition appears fulfilled but entry still fails, use official support with the exact event and displayed condition.

Final session times are displayed in-game under the checked FNCS Solos and Arenas rules. A Rules Library listing alone does not confirm that a session is open now. Recheck the selected event before committing to your practice schedule. [FNCS Solos, section 2.3](https://www.fortnite.com/competitive/rules-guidelines/rules-library/fortnite-championship-series-fncs-solos-2026-official-rules?region=NAC), [Arenas Performance Evaluation, Attachment B](https://www.fortnite.com/competitive/rules-guidelines/rules-library/arenas-performance-evaluation-chapter-7-season-4-official-rules?region=NAC).
