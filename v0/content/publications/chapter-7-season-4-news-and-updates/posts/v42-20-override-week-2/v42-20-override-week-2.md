# v42.20: Override Week, day two

Historical coverage revised October 4, 2026. The reference is [Epic's September 17, 2026 announcement or release note](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/42-20-fortnite-ecosystem-updates-and-release-notes). It dates the update, not this site's original publication day. Read the [season guide](/fortnite/blog/chapter-7-season-4-news-and-updates/start-here).

The second Override Week update arrived on 17 September 2026 and brought the season's biggest collaboration.

## Kingdom Hearts

The Kingdom Key is a melee weapon with combos and lightning. Sea Salt Ice Cream heals. Sora, Riku, Kairi and Roxas outfits, the Gummi Ship, and the music came with it. Power Hours on 19 and 20 September started every player with the Kingdom Key.

## Also new

- Rebecca's Guns from Cyberpunk: Edgerunners, a dual assault rifle and submachine gun with an exotic variant.
- Ironmouse in the Icon Series.
- Four Sprites: Pond, the community winner, Crash Bandicoot, Blinky, and Birthday from 26 September.
- Six Match Overrides: Recovery, Level Up, Dynamic Storm, Super Shot, Big Boom, Healing Overdrive. Suppressing Fire and Health Siphon followed on 24 September.
- A new mid-match objective, the Tetris Tower.
- Loot Hacks refreshed: Deadeye DMR, Hyperburst Pistol, Mammoth Pistol, Reaper Sniper, FlowBerry Fizz, Rocket Ram.
- Free rewards: the Pixel Polli outfit for mastering 30 Sprites, the Pixel Sprite Trainer, and Cocho 9 birthday cake quests.

## Other modes and matchmaking

Gotham City replaced Neo Tilted in Fortnite OG. Surf City returned to Reload. The North American server regions were merged, with a subregion preference in matchmaking.
