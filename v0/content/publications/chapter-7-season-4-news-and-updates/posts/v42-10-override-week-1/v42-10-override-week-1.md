# v42.10: Override Week, day one

Historical coverage revised October 4, 2026. The reference is [Epic's September 3, 2026 announcement or release note](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/42-10-fortnite-ecosystem-updates-and-release-notes). It dates the update, not this site's original publication day. Read the [season guide](/fortnite/blog/chapter-7-season-4-news-and-updates/start-here).

The first mid-season update landed on 3 September 2026. Epic called it Override Week Update Day #1, the first of two.

## New Sprites

| Sprite | Buff |
| --- | --- |
| X-Ray | Marks enemies |
| Onigiri | An override effect after eating |
| Overshield | Extra shield |
| Mega Man | Slide friction |

Mastered Sprites now cost half the Sprite Dust.

## New Match Overrides

Big Head Mode, Prop Hunt, Action Hero, and Sharpshooter, the last one unlocking the following Thursday.

## Loot and balance

Six new Loot Hacks, among them the Caduceus Staff, Scorpion's Combat Kit and the Wrecker Revolver. The Sweeper Shotgun came back. The Pump got a buff, assault rifle headshots a nerf, and the Mega Buster fires faster.

## Other modes

- **Fortnite OG, Season X**: Tilted Town, Starry Suburbs, The Meteor, Dusty Depot, the B.R.U.T.E. mech, Junk Rift and Rift Zones.
- **LEGO Fortnite Odyssey**: a six-week Batman event, Knight Shift, through 15 October, with the Batmobile, Batglider, Bat Grapnel and Bat-Signal.
- **Reload**: a Mini-Venture grants 150 Overshield on an elimination.
