# WoW Forever: Dwarf changes from Classic

Dwarves gain a new class choice and a revised racial toolkit in Blizzard's announced version of World of Warcraft: Forever. The main reference is the September 13, 2026 Deep Dive recap, supplemented by the September 22 character roster. This retrospective draft was written October 4, 2026.

## The Classic baseline

This comparison uses unexpanded WoW Classic, as described in Blizzard's [February 27, 2020 class guide](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23317716/taking-your-first-steps-in-world-of-warcraft-classic). Its Shaman list is Horde-only: Orc, Troll and Tauren. It is a different baseline from Burning Crusade, Season of Discovery or modern WoW.

## What Blizzard announced

| Feature | Classic baseline | Announced for Forever |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shaman access | No Dwarf Shaman. | Dwarf joins the Shaman roster. |
| Stoneform's defensive effect | An Armor increase, according to Blizzard's comparison. | Reduced incoming Physical damage. |
| Find Treasure | Blizzard describes simultaneous tracking as a change. | Can stay enabled alongside other tracking abilities. |

The [September 13 recap](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303313/world-of-warcraft-forever-deep-dive-panel-recap) also retains Stoneform's removal of, and temporary protection against, Bleeds, Poisons and Diseases. It describes Mace Specialization as a critical-chance benefit for spells and abilities while wielding a mace, and Big Game Hunter as extra damage against Beasts.

The [September 22 roster](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24304075/create-the-hero-you-want-to-be-in-world-of-warcraft-forever) gives Stoneform an eight-second duration. It does not specify a damage-reduction percentage or cooldown. Those values remain unknown here.

## What that means when choosing a character

Dwarf Shaman is the clear new choice for someone attached to the race. The tracking change also removes a tradeoff for treasure hunters who use another tracking ability. The mace and Beast bonuses depend on your equipment and target; these announcements alone do not establish a best race for every encounter.

## Beta limits

These are Blizzard's pre-release descriptions, subject to beta changes. This article does not report measured beta behavior, a confirmed bug fix or final launch balance. Recheck current notes before building around an exact racial value.
