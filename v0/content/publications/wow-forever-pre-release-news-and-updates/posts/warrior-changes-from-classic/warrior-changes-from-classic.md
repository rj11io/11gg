# WoW Forever: Warrior changes from Classic

Blizzard's September 30, 2026 Warrior deep dive describes Rage changes, earlier access to stance utility and separate major cooldowns. It also acknowledges beta Rage bugs and announces a further critical-hit adjustment. This retrospective draft was written October 4, 2026; neither a bug report nor a planned change establishes a fix in the current build.

## The Classic baseline

Blizzard's [February 27, 2020 guide](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23317716/taking-your-first-steps-in-world-of-warcraft-classic) lists Warriors for every original Classic race, with tank and damage roles and Arms, Fury and Protection specializations. This is unexpanded Classic, separate from Burning Crusade, Season of Discovery and modern WoW.

Forever's [September 22 roster](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24304075/create-the-hero-you-want-to-be-in-world-of-warcraft-forever) includes Warrior access for both Skyborne variants. The ability comparison below follows Blizzard's explicit original-game changes, without adding unsupported historical formula numbers.

## The announced mechanics

| Area | Description in the September 30 deep dive |
| --- | --- |
| Rage from taking damage | Calculation ignores Armor and damage absorption, so those protections are not intended to reduce the Rage gained. |
| Rage from dealing damage | Normalized around the speed of the weapon delivering the damage. |
| Tactical Mastery | Baseline at level 14, retaining 10 Rage on a stance change; Arms talents can improve it. |
| Victory Rush | Baseline at level 20, with a damage-and-healing attack made available after killing a non-trivial enemy. |
| Major cooldowns | Recklessness, Retaliation and Shield Wall no longer share cooldowns. Retaliation and Shield Wall are described with 15-minute cooldowns. |

The source is the [September 30 Priest and Warrior deep dive](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301514/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-priest-and-warrior#warrior), rechecked October 4. These are design descriptions, rather than independent measurements of a beta client.

## Known beta problem and planned adjustment

In that article, Blizzard says players are experiencing Rage-generation bugs and that the team is investigating their cause. This draft has no later evidence confirming that investigation ended or the bugs were fixed.

The article separately says the initial beta gave no extra outgoing Rage for critical strikes, and announces a return of a critical-hit bonus: 75% more Rage than a non-critical strike. That is the planned adjustment described on September 30. No dated deployment evidence is included here, so it is not presented as verified October 4 behavior.

## Practical implications

The announced stance-retention tool is worth considering when practicing stance changes. Separate major cooldowns invite decisions about when each tool is useful. Neither description proves a rotation, damage ranking or tanking result.

For Rage, distinguish the intended model from what a particular build actually does. If you encounter a mismatch, check the build number and current official notes before treating it as the final design. Beta abilities and talents can continue to change.
