# WoW Forever: Skyborne factions and racials

Skyborne are a new playable race with two faction identities. The Horde's Windshapers follow elemental traditions; the Alliance's High Order pursues its arcane inheritance. Blizzard announced the race on September 12, 2026. This retrospective draft was written October 4 using that panel and the September 22 roster. These are pre-release descriptions, subject to change. [September 12 panel](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303862/world-of-warcraft-forever-whats-next-panel-recap).

## What changes from Classic

The comparison baseline is unexpanded WoW Classic, represented by Blizzard's February 27, 2020 roster guide. That roster has eight races and no Skyborne. This is an added choice, without an old Skyborne toolkit to compare against. [Classic guide](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23317716/taking-your-first-steps-in-world-of-warcraft-classic).

| Choice | Announced classes |
| --- | --- |
| Both factions | Druid, Hunter, Rogue, Warrior |
| Windshapers, Horde | Those four plus Shaman |
| High Order, Alliance | Those four plus Mage |

Faction is chosen at character creation. Blizzard also announced race-specific Druid forms and Zephras Isle as a level 1–12 starting experience. [September 12 panel](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303862/world-of-warcraft-forever-whats-next-panel-recap).

## The announced racial toolkit

| Trait | Both variants |
| --- | --- |
| Walk on Air | Downward glide lasting 10 seconds |
| Wind Blessed | 1% haste for melee, ranged attacks and spellcasting |
| Elemental Insight | 5% additional damage against Elementals |

Windshapers' Skysight grants an elemental blessing with 10% additional run speed. High Order's Read Ley Line activates a ley line for 100% additional Health and Mana regeneration. The roster does not state their cooldowns or durations. Regeneration is distinct from an instant full refill. [September 22 roster](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24304075/create-the-hero-you-want-to-be-in-world-of-warcraft-forever).

The glide is described as downward movement; the panel also rules out flying mounts. Treat it as a movement tool, without assuming unrestricted flight. These announcements do not establish which variant performs better in a particular encounter. [September 12 panel](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303862/world-of-warcraft-forever-whats-next-panel-recap).

## One loss, two responses

Windshapers descend from high elves gifted with elemental skysight. They seek their missing wind mentors and want to restore that relationship. High Order descendants of Eldre'Thalas scholars see dependence on the vanished spirits as a weakness; they seek lost arcane knowledge and self-reliance. Both explanations are announced character backgrounds, without a verified quest ending. [September 22 roster](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24304075/create-the-hero-you-want-to-be-in-world-of-warcraft-forever).

## Access and remaining questions

Blizzard's September 12 announcement lists Skyborne and Zephras Isle access in the optional Skyborne Heroic Pack, inherited by the Epic Pack and Warcraft Forever Collection. Check the current entitlement terms before choosing an edition. [Original announcement](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302093/carve-a-new-path-with-world-of-warcraft-forever).

No current beta build was tested for this article. Starting-quest objectives, prerequisites, rewards, missing active-ability timings and final balance need original game evidence before this becomes a practical quest or build guide.
