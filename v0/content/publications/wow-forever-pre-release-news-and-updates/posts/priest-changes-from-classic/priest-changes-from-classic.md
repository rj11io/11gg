# WoW Forever: Priest changes from Classic

Blizzard's September 30, 2026 Priest deep dive broadens access to core spells while preserving a separate set of race-specific Priest abilities. This retrospective draft was written October 4, 2026. It covers announced beta changes and the September 22 roster detail, with current-build behavior still untested here.

## The Classic baseline

The roster baseline is unexpanded WoW Classic. Blizzard's [February 27, 2020 guide](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23317716/taking-your-first-steps-in-world-of-warcraft-classic) lists Human, Dwarf, Night Elf, Undead and Troll Priests. Forever's [September 22 roster](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24304075/create-the-hero-you-want-to-be-in-world-of-warcraft-forever) adds Gnome.

The spell comparison uses Blizzard's explicit original-game changes. Burning Crusade, Season of Discovery and modern WoW have separate ability histories; their spell access should not be substituted for this baseline.

## Wider access to baseline spells

| Spell | Change described for Forever |
| --- | --- |
| Fear Ward | Available to every Priest at level 20, regardless of race. |
| Devouring Plague | Also available to every Priest at level 20. |
| Divine Spirit | Becomes baseline at level 30 rather than requiring its former talent. |
| Shadow Word: Death | New baseline spell at level 32. Its damage comes with self-damage if the target survives. |

The [September 30 Priest and Warrior deep dive](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301514/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-priest-and-warrior#priest) provides these descriptions. Its healing changes also let Prayer of Healing target another party and allow multiple Priests' Renew effects on one target.

## Priest racial spells remain distinct

The deep dive gives each Priest race two class spells. These belong to the Priest toolkit, separate from the race's general traits such as Stoneform or Cannibalize.

| Priest race | Announced class-specific spells |
| --- | --- |
| Gnome | Confounding Flash confuses nearby enemies; Contingency Plan protects an ally after a health threshold is crossed. |
| Dwarf | Desperate Prayer remains; Chastise damages and immobilizes a Humanoid. |
| Human | Divine Grace heals a low-health ally and removes Weakened Soul; Feedback remains. |
| Night Elf | Starshards gains damage and a cooldown; Elune's Grace changes attack avoidance. |
| Troll | Hex of Weakness and Shadowguard remain unchanged in this account. |
| Undead | Touch of Weakness remains; Dark Sacrifice trades the Priest's health for Mana over time. |

These are the [same deep dive's Priest racial descriptions](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301514/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-priest-and-warrior#priest), rechecked October 4. Dark Sacrifice is a class spell; the general Undead Cannibalize trait uses corpses. Compare those two systems separately when choosing a character.

## Practical implications and beta limits

Universal spell access gives you more freedom to consider a race for its other tools. The remaining Priest-specific spells still matter, so inspect their conditions rather than assuming every Priest has an identical kit. Shadow Word: Death's self-damage also deserves attention when deciding whether to use it on a surviving target.

This is an announcement comparison, without a measured healing ranking, a confirmed bug fix or proof of deployment in the latest build. Abilities and talents remain subject to beta change. Recheck current notes before treating a target condition, cooldown or interaction as final.
