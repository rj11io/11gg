# WoW Forever: Undead changes from Classic

Blizzard's announced Undead changes combine a new class option with a different racial tradeoff. This comparison covers the September 13, 2026 Deep Dive recap and September 22 roster. It was written as a retrospective draft on October 4, 2026.

## The Classic baseline

The baseline is unexpanded WoW Classic. Blizzard's [February 27, 2020 guide](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23317716/taking-your-first-steps-in-world-of-warcraft-classic) restricts Paladins to Alliance Humans and Dwarves. Burning Crusade, Season of Discovery and modern WoW are separate comparisons.

## The announced changes

| Feature | Classic baseline | Announced for Forever |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paladin access | No Undead Paladin. | Undead joins the Paladin roster. |
| Will of the Forsaken | Removes control effects and grants immunity, as described by Blizzard's comparison. | Still breaks Charm, Fear and Sleep, without the immunity afterward. |
| Cannibalize | Health recovery. | Restores Mana too. |
| Underwater Breathing | Existing underwater utility. | Retained without a change, according to the recap. |

The [September 13 recap](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303313/world-of-warcraft-forever-deep-dive-panel-recap) is the source for those old-to-new racial comparisons. No original immunity duration or racial cooldown is inferred from it.

The [September 22 roster](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24304075/create-the-hero-you-want-to-be-in-world-of-warcraft-forever) lists Undead Paladins and Touch of the Grave: a passive chance for attacks to drain enemy health. A chance to trigger is different from healing on every attack.

## Choosing an Undead character

For an Undead Paladin, the attraction begins with being able to choose that combination at all. Mana recovery also matters to mana-using characters. The Will of the Forsaken change deserves particular attention: breaking a control effect no longer promises protection against a new one afterward.

Those are reasons to inspect the toolkit before choosing a character, rather than treating the race as an automatic upgrade. The announcements do not establish a best race, final damage output or a complete Paladin story.

## Beta limits

This is pre-release reporting, subject to beta changes. Live behavior, bug fixes and final launch balance have not been measured here. No quest outcome is claimed. Check current beta notes before relying on an exact value or cooldown.
