# WoW Forever: Druid changes from Classic

Blizzard's September 30, 2026 Druid deep dive describes fewer reasons to leave a shapeshift, equipment changes and new baseline abilities. This retrospective draft was written October 4, 2026. It covers announced changes, with planned beta talents kept separate.

## The Classic baseline

The roster baseline is unexpanded WoW Classic: Blizzard's [February 27, 2020 guide](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23317716/taking-your-first-steps-in-world-of-warcraft-classic) lists Night Elf and Tauren Druids. Forever's [September 22 roster](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24304075/create-the-hero-you-want-to-be-in-world-of-warcraft-forever) adds Skyborne on both factions.

The ability comparison follows Blizzard's explicit original-game changes in the September 30 deep dive. It does not import Burning Crusade, Season of Discovery or modern WoW mechanics into that baseline.

## The announced ability changes

| Area | Change described for Forever |
| --- | --- |
| Consumables | Potions and most similar items can be used without leaving form. |
| Equipment | Weapon enchantments and procs work in form. Bear, Cat and Dire Bear auto-attacks use the equipped weapon's DPS, with attack speed set by the form. |
| Interactions | Most doors and objects, plus racial abilities, can be used while shifted. |
| Resurrection | New Revive at level 12 resurrects outside combat. |
| Baseline abilities | Nature's Grasp at 10 and Omen of Clarity at 20 no longer require talents. Lacerate is a new baseline ability at 42. |

Those descriptions come from the [September 30 Hunter and Druid deep dive](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301515/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-hunter-and-druid#druid). Engineering remains an exception: most bombs cannot be used in form, with specific SAF-T and EZ-Thro recipes allowed.

## Talents awaiting a build

That same article describes Shifting Power as replacing Tiger's Fury, but explicitly says the talent is not yet in beta. It also marks Improved Shifting Power and the Shredding Attacks row move as pending.

Those are plans as of September 30. This draft has no later deployment evidence, so it does not call them available on October 4. Check current notes before choosing a build around them.

## What matters when playing

The practical appeal is fewer interruptions to a chosen form. The equipment change also gives you another reason to inspect the weapon you equip. Revive's out-of-combat restriction matters when deciding what a group can recover from; it is not a promise of another combat resurrection.

Beta abilities remain subject to change. This article records Blizzard's descriptions, without measured performance, a confirmed bug fix or final launch balance.
