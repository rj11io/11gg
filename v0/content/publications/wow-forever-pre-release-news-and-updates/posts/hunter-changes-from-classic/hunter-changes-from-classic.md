# WoW Forever: Hunter changes from Classic

Blizzard's September 30, 2026 Hunter deep dive describes broader access to Aimed Shot, combat traps and a revised pet toolkit. This retrospective draft was written October 4, 2026. It records announced beta changes, without claiming tested performance or final launch balance.

## The Classic baseline

The roster baseline is unexpanded WoW Classic. Blizzard's [February 27, 2020 guide](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23317716/taking-your-first-steps-in-world-of-warcraft-classic) lists Night Elf, Dwarf, Orc, Tauren and Troll Hunters, with damage as their group role. Forever's [September 22 roster](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24304075/create-the-hero-you-want-to-be-in-world-of-warcraft-forever) adds Human and both factions' Skyborne.

The mechanics below follow Blizzard's explicit original-game comparisons. Burning Crusade, Season of Discovery and modern WoW are separate versions, with different abilities and talent histories.

## The announced changes

| Area | Change described for Forever |
| --- | --- |
| Aimed Shot | Becomes baseline at level 20, leaving the talent tree. Arcane Shot no longer shares its cooldown; Multi-Shot now does. |
| Traps | Can be placed during combat, with a 30-second cooldown. Frost and Fire traps have separate cooldowns. |
| Pet scaling | Pet stats scale with the Hunter's gear. Player buffs that increase pet stats no longer apply. |
| Pet families | Foxes join the available families. Families gain distinct abilities and broader access to Bite or Claw and Dive or Dash. |

These are Blizzard's descriptions in the [September 30 Hunter and Druid deep dive](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301515/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-hunter-and-druid#hunter), rechecked October 4. They establish the announcement, rather than proving every feature works in a particular beta build.

## Builds and limits

Beast Mastery develops companion play; Marksmanship focuses on ranged attacks; Survival offers trap and melee options. Consider which part of the toolkit you want to practice before investing talent points. This comparison does not rank their damage or promise a best build.

The deep dive retains the ranged dead zone within eight yards and traps placed at the Hunter's own location. Combat traps therefore still require positioning. A pet can tank during solo or small-group play; the Hunter's stated combat role remains damage. That pet utility does not establish a new player raid-tank role.

## What to recheck

Treat this as a dated beta snapshot. Abilities and talents can change, and no independent live testing or later deployment evidence is included here. Check current build notes before relying on a cooldown, family ability or talent interaction.
