# Forsaken Kingdom: the story bridge to WoW Forever

Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom fills the space between Lordaeron's fall and the Forsaken's place in early Azeroth. Blizzard's September 12, 2026 announcement connects that campaign to World of Warcraft: Forever. This retrospective draft was written October 4 from the announcement and panel recap, without an independent campaign playthrough. [Original announcement](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301511/wage-war-once-more-with-warcraft-iii-reforged-forsaken-kingdom).

## Where the bridge fits

| Stage | What the original sources establish |
| --- | --- |
| Arthas returns to Lordaeron | The Last Days of Lordaeron prologue revisits the kingdom's final hours through its guards |
| Forsaken Kingdom | The main campaign explores Undercity's founding and the Forsaken joining the Horde |
| WoW Forever | The new branch begins after Forsaken Kingdom and before Molten Core |

The Warcraft III panel describes a four-year gap between Warcraft III and World of Warcraft. The Forever announcement gives the later starting boundary; neither establishes an exact calendar date for every mission. [Warcraft III panel](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302500/warcraft-iii-reforged-forsaken-kingdom-what-s-next-panel-recap), [Forever announcement](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302093/carve-a-new-path-with-world-of-warcraft-forever).

## Familiar history through new eyes

The panel introduces Garek Bandarion, a newly risen Forsaken warrior, and Dark Ranger Anya. Their perspectives sit alongside familiar figures such as Sylvanas. Blizzard presents this as a chance to explore how the Forsaken rebuild a community beneath Lordaeron and retain their freedom from the Scourge. Those are announced story subjects; the recap does not reveal a complete mission-by-mission resolution. [Warcraft III panel](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302500/warcraft-iii-reforged-forsaken-kingdom-what-s-next-panel-recap).

The original World of Warcraft manual already describes Sylvanas leading the Forsaken, occupying Undercity and aligning with the Horde. The new campaign adds viewpoints and detail around an established destination. Calling something a retcon requires an actual contradiction between original and new evidence; an added perspective alone does not establish one. See the manual's Undead/Forsaken and Undercity entries. [Original manual, copyright 2004–2005](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/template_resource/263A8NGR8HLZ1556919642368.pdf).

## The Forsaken Paladin thread

Blizzard says the campaign introduces the Forsaken Paladin's origins, with threads continuing into Forever. The Warcraft III Tavern hero and a player-character Paladin in WoW are different systems; the shared name does not prove identical abilities. [Warcraft III panel](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302500/warcraft-iii-reforged-forsaken-kingdom-what-s-next-panel-recap).

For the class comparison, the baseline is unexpanded WoW Classic. Blizzard's February 27, 2020 guide lists Human and Dwarf Paladins; the September 22, 2026 Forever roster adds Undead Paladins. This documents expanded access, without establishing every step of their narrative origin. [Classic guide](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23317716/taking-your-first-steps-in-world-of-warcraft-classic), [Forever roster](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24304075/create-the-hero-you-want-to-be-in-world-of-warcraft-forever).

## What this establishes

The sources support an announced continuity bridge and new playable viewpoints. They do not verify every dialogue choice, quest, ending or change to Classic lore. A complete narrative comparison needs versioned campaign scenes and original quest text, checked side by side. Forever remains in beta at this draft's cutoff; its eventual quest implementation and later revisions require a fresh evidence check.
