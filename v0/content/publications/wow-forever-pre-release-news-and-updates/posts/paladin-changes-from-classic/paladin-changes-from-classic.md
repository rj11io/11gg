# WoW Forever: Paladin changes from Classic

Blizzard's September 13, 2026 deep-dive recap introduces new Paladin tools while retaining gaps in the class toolkit. This retrospective draft was written October 4, 2026. It covers that announcement and the September 22 roster detail; beta descriptions can change before launch.

## The Classic baseline

Blizzard's [February 27, 2020 Classic guide](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23317716/taking-your-first-steps-in-world-of-warcraft-classic) lists Human and Dwarf Paladins, restricted to the Alliance. It names Holy, Protection and Retribution and the healing, tanking and damage roles. This is unexpanded Classic, without importing Burning Crusade, Season of Discovery or modern WoW mechanics.

Forever's [September 22 roster](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24304075/create-the-hero-you-want-to-be-in-world-of-warcraft-forever) adds Undead Paladins on the Horde. That changes who can choose the class; racial traits and Paladin spells still need to be considered separately.

## The announced changes

| Tool | Change described for Forever |
| --- | --- |
| Holy Strike | New instant Holy weapon strike at level 6, with a 12-second cooldown. |
| Seals and Judgment | Casting Judgment no longer consumes the active Seal. |
| Seal of Fury | Tank-focused Seal with small absorb shields. Judgment taunts while this Seal is active. |
| Consecration | Becomes baseline at level 20. Damage and threat are stronger on the first four targets, with reduced effects beyond them. |

These descriptions come from [A Closer Look at Paladins in the September 13 recap](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303313/world-of-warcraft-forever-deep-dive-panel-recap), rechecked October 4. The taunt has a Seal prerequisite; it is not an unconditional replacement for every tank's taunt button.

## Specialization opportunities

The recap describes Holy options for varied healing, Protection tools for Mana and survival, and Retribution paths for melee or hybrid Holy damage. A useful first question is which role you want to practice, then which talents support its recurring decisions. More tools alone do not demonstrate equivalent performance across roles.

For a tank, the Seal and Judgment relationship is worth learning before a pull. For a healer or damage dealer, baseline Consecration adds another option, but its target scaling still matters. These are practical implications of the descriptions, rather than measured beta results.

## Retained limits and beta uncertainty

The same recap explicitly retains the lack of an interrupt and a target slow, with fewer ranged options against enemies other than Undead and Demons. A group still needs to cover those gaps. New specialization tools do not make every utility interchangeable.

This article does not verify a live build, a resolved bug or final launch balance. Recheck current official notes before selecting a build around these mechanics, especially any changed cooldown or talent interaction.
