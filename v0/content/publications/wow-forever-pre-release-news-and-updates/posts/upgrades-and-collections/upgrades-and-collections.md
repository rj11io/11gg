# Upgrades, packs and the Collector's Edition

Historical coverage revised October 4, 2026. The reference date is September 12, 2026, for [Blizzard’s BlizzCon announcement](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302093/carve-a-new-path-with-world-of-warcraft-forever); the overview incorporates later sources linked below. This date identifies the event being covered, not this site’s original publication day. Start with the [pre-release guide](/world-of-warcraft/forever/blog/wow-forever-pre-release-news-and-updates/start-here).

The game itself comes with a subscription. What Blizzard sells on top are packs and a collection, opened for pre-purchase alongside the announcement.

## The three upgrades

| Upgrade | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| Skyborne Heroic Pack | Cosmetics tied to the new Skyborne race |
| Skyborne Epic Pack | Everything in Heroic, plus beta access and 30 days of game time |
| Warcraft Forever Collection | The Epic Pack plus Warcraft III Reforged and its Forsaken Kingdom campaign |

The collection is on sale through 11 January 2027. A physical Collector's Edition is sold through the Blizzard Gear store.

## What you do not need to buy

Nothing. A subscription is enough to play World of Warcraft: Forever on 4 November. The Epic Pack was the only way into the beta besides an invite, which is the practical reason it sold.

## The Forsaken Kingdom link

Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom released on the same day as the announcement. Forever's story starts right after it, which is why the collection bundles the two: one story, two games.
