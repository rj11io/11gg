# FNCS Global Last Chance 2026: the final ten Global places

Global Last Chance completed the Duos route to Antwerp after Summit and Major 2. It was a separate event, distinct from the Last Chance Qualifier inside each Major.

Historical coverage written October 3, 2026, using August 14 as the organizer's Finals reference date. Results were checked October 3. Start with the [2026 event guide]({{indexHref}}).

## August's qualification route

The first qualifier's three rounds ran August 3-5; the second ran August 7-9. Each qualifier sent 25 Duos to a shared 50-team final on August 14. Regional sessions can finish on the next date in another timezone.

The final played six matches. Europe received three Global places, NA Central two, and Brazil, Asia, Oceania, Middle East and NA West one each: ten places altogether.
