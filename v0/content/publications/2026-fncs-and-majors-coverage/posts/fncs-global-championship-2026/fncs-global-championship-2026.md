# FNCS Global Championship 2026: Pixie and SwizzY win by two points

Pixie and SwizzY won the 2026 Global Championship with 525 points. Shxrk and t3eny finished two points behind; Malibuca and Vico were another two points back.

Historical coverage written October 3, 2026, using September 27 as the Finals reference date. The cumulative final leaderboard was checked October 3. See the [2026 event guide]({{indexHref}}) for the preceding qualification routes.

## Antwerp's international final

The championship ran September 26-27 at Lotto Arena in Antwerp, Belgium. Fifty Duos played 12 matches across two days for a $2 million prize pool. This was the international Duos final after Summit, Major 2 and Global Last Chance.
