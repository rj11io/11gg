# FNCS Major 1 2026: the route to Summit

FNCS Major 1 opened the 2026 Duos circuit with regional competition and a route to the international Summit. Europe's final leaderboard was led by AG Scroll 10ǃ and AG Sky.; NA Central's by XSET Curve1x and xset νeno.

Historical coverage written October 3, 2026. The reference date is the organizer's April 26 Finals date; the standings below were checked on October 3. Read the [2026 event guide]({{indexHref}}) for the full circuit.

## Dates and format

Epic's calendar placed Play-Ins on April 6-7, Heats on April 12, 18 and 19, the Last Chance Qualifier on April 20-21, and Finals on April 25-26. Regional sessions can cross midnight in other timezones.

Division 1 teams entered Play-Ins, then advanced through Heats or the Last Chance route. The Finals combined six matches per day into a 12-match score. Qualification here led to the Summit Upper stage, rather than directly to Global Championship.

## What came next

Major 1 supplied 50 Summit Upper-stage places. The separate Second Chance Qualifier supplied another 25 teams to the Lower stage. Both routes met at the Summit in May.
