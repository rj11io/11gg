# FNCS Solos 2026: the October schedule and route to Finals

FNCS Solos is the standalone regional competition after the Duos season. As of October 3, it is ongoing; final results are pending.

## Route to the regional final

Qualifier 1 sends 100 players to Fast Track. Its top eight reach Finals directly; the other 92 join 108 players from Qualifier 2 in Heats. Each of the two Heats advances six match winners and the next 29 on points. Last Chance adds five match winners and the next 17 on points.

That produces 100 finalists per region: eight from Fast Track, 70 from Heats and 22 from Last Chance. Finals combine six matches on each day, for 12 matches total.

This article will be updated with checked final standings after the event. It currently records the schedule and format, without predictions or provisional champions.
