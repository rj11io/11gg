# FNCS Second Chance 2026: another route to Summit

The Major 1 Second Chance Qualifier gave Division 1 Duos another route to the Summit. It was a separate event with its own final, not a third Major or the Major's earlier Last Chance Qualifier.

Historical coverage written October 3, 2026, using April 29 as the organizer's final reference date. Leaderboards were checked on October 3. Read the [2026 event guide]({{indexHref}}) for the season's qualification routes.

## Dates and qualification

The organizer calendar ran April 28-29. A Play-In selected 50 teams per region for a six-match final. Across the regions, 25 places led to Summit's Lower stage: eight from Europe, seven from NA Central and two each from Brazil, Asia, Oceania, Middle East and NA West.

Entering the Lower stage gave those teams a further chance to reach the Summit Grand Finals; it did not itself award a Global Championship place.

## Why the event mattered

The Summit field combined Major 1's 50 Upper-stage qualifiers and Second Chance's 25 Lower-stage qualifiers. Second Chance therefore changed which teams reached the international event without creating another Major title.
