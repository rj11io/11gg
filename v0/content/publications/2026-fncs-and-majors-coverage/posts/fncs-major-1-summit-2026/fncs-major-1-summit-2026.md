# FNCS Summit 2026: Malibuca and Vico win in Düsseldorf

Malibuca and Vico finished the Major 1 Summit Grand Finals on 406 points, ahead of Pollo and Peterbot on 374. The international event joined Major 1's regional routes and awarded the first 15 places at Global Championship.

Historical coverage written October 3, 2026, with May 31 as its Finals reference date. Results were checked on October 3. Start with the [2026 event guide]({{indexHref}}).

## Dates and format

The Summit ran May 29-31 in Düsseldorf, Germany, with 75 Duos and a $1.3 million prize pool stated in Epic's event overview.

The Upper stage played six matches on May 29, sending 25 teams to the Grand Finals. The Lower stage played six on May 30; its six match winners and next 19 teams on points filled the other 25 places. The 50-team Grand Finals played eight matches on May 31. Scores in the table below are Grand Finals scores, not totals across all stages.

## Grand Finals podium
