# FNCS Major 2 2026: the summer route to Global

FNCS Major 2 returned to regional Duos competition in July and August. Its 25 Global Championship places followed the Summit's 15, leaving the final ten places to Global Last Chance.

Historical coverage written October 3, 2026. August 2 is the organizer's final reference date; results were checked on October 3. Read the [2026 event guide]({{indexHref}}) for the complete circuit.

## The revised calendar

Epic moved Major 2 forward in its May announcement. Play-Ins ran July 18-19, Heats July 24-26, the Major's Last Chance Qualifier July 27-28, and Finals August 1-2. Regional sessions can finish on the next date in another timezone.

The regional Finals combined 12 matches over two days. Nine Global places came from Europe, six from NA Central, and two each from Brazil, Asia, Oceania, Middle East and NA West.
