## Dates that moved

- Onimusha: Way of the Sword, 25 September to 4 September, earlier. Capcom investor notice, 2 July.
- Phantom Blade Zero, 9 September to 29 October.
- Lords of the Fallen II, September to the first quarter of 2027. CI Games said it was avoiding the crowd.
- Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War IV, 17 September to 3 December, PC.
- Aliens: Fireteam Elite 2 on Switch 2, 26 September to early 2027, after layoffs at Cold Iron on 29 September.
- Steins;Gate Re:Boot on Xbox, to 29 October with the other consoles.

- 25 August: Gamescom Opening Night Live. 26 to 30 August: Gamescom, Cologne.
