# Launch: version 1.0.2, 8 April 2026

Historical coverage revised October 4, 2026. The reference is the April 8, 2026 [launch or update date](https://asia-press.portal-pokemon.com/press-release/pokemon-champions_20260324/), rather than this site’s original publication day. Read the [2026 update guide](/pokemon/champions/blog/2026-pokemon-champions-news-and-updates/start-here).

Pokémon Champions launched on Nintendo Switch and Switch 2 on 8 April 2026, the evening of 7 April in North America, as a free-to-start battle game. Version 1.0.2 was the launch build; no official notes exist for it, so the number comes from community trackers.

## What the launch had

- 186 Pokémon.
- Three ways to play: Ranked, Casual, and Private battles with online tournaments.
- Mega Evolution from day one. Terastallization was described as coming later, with no date.
- Save data tied to a Nintendo Account, ready for the mobile versions that followed.
- Switch 2 gets better visuals through a free update, not a separate edition.

## Paid options at launch

| Option | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Starter Pack | 50 extra storage slots, the Let's Go battle song, 30 Teammate Tickets, 50 Training Tickets |
| Premium Battle Pass | The paid track of each season's pass |
| Membership | A recurring plan |

## Ranked from day one

Regulation M-A and Season M-1 started on launch day. Season M-1 ran to 13 May, with the first Battle Pass on the same dates.

## The first week

A day after launch the developers posted a notice about bugs: Leech Seed text, turn order when both sides Mega Evolve, the tutorial's gender choice, Lightning Rod with Encore, and a cursor lock after Mega Evolving. The fixes came in 1.0.3.
