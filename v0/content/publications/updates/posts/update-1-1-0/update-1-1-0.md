# Update 1.1.0, 17 June 2026: mobile and Regulation M-B

Version 1.1.0 arrived with the iOS and Android launch on 17 June 2026. One Nintendo Account carries the save across every platform. The same day, Regulation M-B replaced M-A.

## What was added

The official notes are brief: the Pokémon and held items for M-B, plus visual and network fixes. The community count puts it at 38 Pokémon and 22 Mega forms, among them Annihilape, Staraptor, Sceptile, Swampert, and Mega Raichu X and Y. Two new abilities: Eelevate and Fire Mane.

## Balance

- Rage Fist: its power resets when the user switches out.
- Make It Rain changed.
- Learnset changes across the roster.

## Gifts

- Raichu with Raichunite X and Y as a login gift from 17 June to 1 September.
- A quick-coupon code, HAJ1MAR1G1FT, for 100 coupons, valid to 31 August.

## Seasons under M-B

| Season | Dates | Battle Pass highlights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M-3 | 17 Jun to 8 Jul | Canari outfit, Mega Stones for Eelektross, Metagross, Starmie |
| M-4 | 8 Jul to 5 Aug | Free Sceptile and Sceptilite, premium Swampert, Blaziken, Meganium outfit |
| M-5 | 5 Aug to 9 Sep | Free Chandelure and Chandelurite, premium Excadrill, Scrafty, Corbeau outfit |

Monthly Championship Series events ran in each season, with Tyranitar, Mawile and Alolan Raichu as rewards.
