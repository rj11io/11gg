# Update 1.0.3, 23 April 2026

The first patch, two weeks after launch, fixed the bugs the developers had listed on day two. Required for online play.

## Fixed

- Leech Seed text.
- The tutorial's gender choice.
- The cursor lock after Mega Evolving when B was pressed.
- Unnerve not blocking Berries.
- Speed changes from held items not applied to ability order.
- Network and visual fixes.

## No balance changes

This was a fix-only update. Regulation M-A and Season M-1 continued unchanged; Season M-2 started on 13 May with the Paxton outfit and Mega Stones for Skarmory, Kangaskhan and Gengar in its Battle Pass.
