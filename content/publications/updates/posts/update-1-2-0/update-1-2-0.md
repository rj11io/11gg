# Update 1.2.0, 9 September 2026: Regulation M-C and Z Mega Evolutions

Announced at the Worlds 2026 closing ceremony on 30 August and released on 9 September (the evening of 8 September Pacific), version 1.2.0 brought Regulation M-C, 24 Pokémon, and the first Z Mega Evolutions. Required for online play.

## New Pokémon

Wigglytuff, Persian, Farfetch'd, Mr. Mime, Salamence, Gogoat, Golisopod, Rillaboom, Cinderace, Inteleon, Thievul, Toxtricity, Grapploct, Perrserker, Sirfetch'd, Pincurchin, Indeedee, Pawmot, Arboliva, Squawkabilly, Mabosstiff, Baxcalibur, and two more to round out the 24.

## New Mega Evolutions

- Mega Salamence, Mega Golisopod, Mega Baxcalibur.
- The first Z Megas: Absol Z, Garchomp Z, Lucario Z.

## New features

- **Battle Log**: a record of what happened in the battle.
- **Selection Support**: a type matchup summary on switch-in, built from ranked data.
- A new battle backdrop.

## Balance

- Politoed loses Pound.
- Archaludon loses Mirror Coat and Metal Burst.
- Wish and Strength Sap drop from 12 PP to 8.

## Regulation M-C

Runs 9 September to 2 December 2026. Season M-6 is the first under it, 9 September to 7 October. Heatran was teased for a later update with no date. Pokémon Champions Junior, a version without in-app purchases, launched the same day on the eShop in Germany and Brazil.
