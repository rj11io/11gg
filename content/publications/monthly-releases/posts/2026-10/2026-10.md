# Gaming releases: October 2026

October is the month everything that fled September landed in, with Gears of War, Call of Duty and a new Final Fantasy stacked in three weeks. November is the opposite: Grand Theft Auto VI on the 19th cleared the calendar, and the only big name willing to stand near it is Zelda. September, now behind us, delivered what it promised.

## Dates that moved

- Grand Theft Auto VI, 26 May 2026 to 19 November 2026, after an earlier move from autumn 2025.
- Fable, to February 2027, out of November.
- Rayman Legends Retold, listed 1 October by two calendars and 3 December by a third. Likely delayed, unconfirmed.
- Phantom Blade Zero, 9 September to 29 October, now landed.
- Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War IV, 17 September to 3 December.

## Events

- 29 October: the Nintendo Switch 2 Zelda 40th Anniversary Edition console, no game bundled.
- 4 November: World of Warcraft: Forever launches at 3:00 pm Pacific, one global time.
- 19 November: Grand Theft Auto VI. Publishers cleared the month around it.
