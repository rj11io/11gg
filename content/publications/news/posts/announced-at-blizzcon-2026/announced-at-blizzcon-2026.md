# WoW Forever announced at BlizzCon 2026

Blizzard opened BlizzCon 2026 on 12 September with a third branch of World of Warcraft. World of Warcraft: Forever sits next to modern WoW and WoW Classic as a permanent version of the original 2004 Azeroth that grows sideways instead of up.

## What it is

- Original Azeroth, level cap stays at 60. New content adds zones, quests, dungeons and raids at that cap instead of raising it.
- The story sits after Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom and before Molten Core.
- Included with a normal World of Warcraft subscription. No separate purchase for the base game.
- Launch: 4 November 2026 at 3:00 pm Pacific, one global launch, no early access.

## What ships at launch

| Piece | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| New race | Skyborne, an elven race, starting on Zephras Isle |
| New zones | Mount Hyjal, Zephras Isle, Riverglades |
| Revamped zones | Three classic zones reworked |
| Quests | About 1,000 new quests across levels 1 to 60 |
| Dungeons | Nine |
| Raids | New 10 and 20 player raids |
| Battleground | Darkspear Islands |
| Race and class combinations | Forsaken Paladin, Dwarf Shaman, among others |

## Why it matters

Players have asked for a "Classic Plus" since Classic launched in 2019: the old world, kept, with new things to do in it. Blizzard said no for years. This is the yes, and it is a permanent branch rather than a season.
