# The beta is live

Five days after the announcement, on 17 September 2026, the World of Warcraft: Forever beta opened. It runs until 21 October.

## Who is in

- Owners of the Skyborne Epic Pack or the Warcraft Forever Collection.
- Invited players. Friend invite codes start going out on 20 October, the day before the beta closes.
- A Server Slam, an open beta weekend to load test the realms, is planned. No date yet.

## What is testable

The beta opened with a level cap of 20 and raised it to 30 later. That covers the Skyborne starting experience on Zephras Isle and the first of the reworked zones. The endgame content, raids and the new battleground are not in the beta.

## The live Q&A

Blizzard ran a live question and answer session on the day the beta opened, covering the design brief: the old world kept intact, new content at the cap, and no plan to raise the cap.
