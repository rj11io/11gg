# v42.30: Fortnitemares 2026, The Game Is Cursed

Fortnitemares started on 1 October 2026 with update v42.30 and runs to 1 November, the earliest start the event has had in years.

## Five Nights at Freddy's

The headline collaboration. Freddy, Bonnie, Chica and Foxy with Withered styles, and Freddy Fazbear's Pizzeria as a new point of interest with its own bosses.

## The rest of the horror roster

A Nightmare on Elm Street puts Freddy Krueger at the new Nightmare Neighborhood. Hazbin Hotel, Beetlejuice, Obsession, Slender Man, Black Clover and The Purge are confirmed. Predator and Hollow Knight were leaked, not confirmed. Battlewoods became Gravegate.

## Weapons and items

- New: Bonerattler SMG, which reveals enemies through walls, and Freddy's Nightmare, a teleport and slash.
- Returning: Wood Stake Shotgun, Pumpkin Launcher, Dual Fiend Hunters, Witch Broom, Heavy AR.

## Overrides and Sprites

Fortnitemares Overrides: Spooky Sprinter, Berserk, Spooky Prop Hunt. Sprites: Spooky Dash, Vampire, The Deer, Dumpster Dive, Pumpkin, with Honey and Obsession Sprites due 15 October.

## Free reward and modes

Jack-A-Nape is the free outfit, earned through 26 event quests. From 8 October: Horde Rush, Blitz Royale, and Reload with horror loadouts. On PC an Epic Quality Assets option arrived, and bandwidth now favours gameplay over cosmetic streaming.

## What comes next

No live event is confirmed. Every Chapter 7 season so far ended with one, so one is expected around 31 October, the battle pass end date. Dataminers point at an update on 15 October, a mini-season from 1 November, and Chapter 8 in early December. None of that is official.
