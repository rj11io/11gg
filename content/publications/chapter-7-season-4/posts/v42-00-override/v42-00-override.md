# v42.00: Override, the season begins

Chapter 7 Season 4 went live on 20 August 2026 with update v42.00. Its name is Override, its tagline "Break the Rules. Change the Game." The island has merged with retro game worlds, a villain called Geno threatens system collapse, and players hack the island back one match at a time.

## The map

Three new points of interest circle a new Zero Point tower at the centre.

| New | Replaces | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green Hill Zone | Calamari Canyon | A Sonic level, loops included |
| Reality's Reign | Frosted Flats | An industrial fortress |
| Stone Sanctum | Sinister Strip | An ancient temple with the Mega Maze |

Smaller landmarks: Epic Mega Arcade, Barncade, Pac-Man Island with Blinky and Pinky Islands, Geno's Machine, Pixel Polys, and a Tetris spot at Sunken Shores.

## The mechanics

- **Sprites**: a new generation of companion creatures, each with a buff. Sonic gives speed, Tails hovers, Shadow reloads for you. The old Sprites moved to a Sprite Garden mode. Sprite Dust is the currency around them.
- **Cheat Codes and Override Consoles**: type a code at a console and the whole match changes, Sonic Speed, More XP, Extra Life, Loot Hack, Big Head and more.
- **Loot Hacks**: spend Sprite Dust to change what chests drop.
- **Lobby Hacks**: real codes typed in the lobby menu for rewards, posted by Epic on social media.
- **Rift Anomalies**: special event drops.

## Weapons and items at launch

Sonic Power Sneakers, Mega Buster, 8-Bit Shotgun, Midas' Masterpiece (a one-shot headshot pistol), Tetris Rift (a healing area), 1-Up Token, God Mode Llama.

## Battle pass

Sonic the Hedgehog is the instant unlock, with a Super Sonic style at level 10 and Tails as a sidekick. The eight outfits run Bastian, Grace Crowne, K1ttyW1ns, Wrixel, Mali, Phantom and Geno at the final tier. Collaborations announced for the season: Mega Man, Pac-Man, Kingdom Hearts, Persona 5 Royal, Crash Bandicoot, Spyro, Tetris, Cyberpunk: Edgerunners, and Ironmouse in the Icon Series.
